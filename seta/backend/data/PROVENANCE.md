## Shape

`hsk.json` is a flat array, sorted by level then frequency:

```json
{"s": "爱", "lv": 1, "q": 382, "py": "ài", "m": "to love; affection"}
```

- `s` simplified form (the only key the app matches on)
- `lv` HSK 3.0 level, 1–7 (7 is the published 7–9 band)
- `q` frequency rank — lower is more common; 1000000 means unranked
- `py` tone-marked pinyin, lowercased for consistency
- `m` English gloss: up to 2 senses within 60 runes, cut at a sense boundary

## Filtering

Old HSK 2.0 bands (`o1`…`o6`) are dropped. Words listed under several
HSK 3.0 bands keep their lowest level, so every simplified form appears once
and per-level counts do not double count.

## Readings and glosses

A word with several readings keeps the first one whose senses say something
useful, so 路 is the road, not `surname Lu`, and 钱 is money, not `surname Qian`.
Senses starting with `surname`, `variant of`, `used in`, `see` or `abbr. for` are
pushed behind the ordinary ones. The pinyin still describes one reading only:
行 is háng here, which is right for 银行 and wrong for 行动.
